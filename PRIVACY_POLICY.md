Privacy Policy for MobileView Pro
Effective Date: October 13, 2026

MobileView Pro ("the Extension") is committed to protecting user privacy. This policy outlines our data collection and processing practices.

1. No Data Collection or Transmission
The Extension operates entirely locally within your Google Chrome browser sandbox. We do not track, collect, store, log, or transmit any user data, browsing history, form inputs, or personally identifiable information to external third-party servers.

2. Permissions Utilization
The Extension requests the 'declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess' permission alongside host access permissions strictly to perform local, per-tab session header modifications. This is utilized solely to clear frame-restriction constraints (such as X-Frame-Options) to enable local or remote web development projects to safely render inside the simulated device frames. No analytical properties or persistent tracking cookies are deployed through this mechanism.

3. Changes to This Policy
We reserve the right to modify this privacy statement at any time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date within the Extension's distribution manifest notes.
